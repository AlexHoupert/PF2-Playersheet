# Game-icons.net Attribution

The local SVG icons in this folder were downloaded from https://game-icons.net
for the Player mobile navigation.

Game-icons.net states that its icons are provided under the Creative Commons
3.0 BY license and may be used with attribution.

Attribution format from Game-icons.net:

> Icons made by {author}. Available on https://game-icons.net
